Architecture & Features

Engineered for Absolute Security

Zero-Knowledge cryptography meets friction-free hardware biometrics. Explore the complete FlakeSecure security suite.

AES-256-CTR • Argon2id • Secure Enclave

Client-Side Encrypted Vault

Your master passwords, credentials, notes, and 2FA secrets reside exclusively on your physical smartphone, encrypted using 256-bit AES in Counter mode with HMAC-SHA256 authentication.

Keys are derived locally with Argon2id using memory-hard parameters. The central server stores only ciphertext blobs and mathematically cannot inspect, decrypt, or log your credentials.

  • • Instant hardware unlock via Apple Face ID, Touch ID, or Android BiometricPrompt
  • • Category management with official SVG security iconography
  • • Complete zero-knowledge cloud backup synchronization
Ephemeral WebSockets • WSS / TLS 1.3

1-Tap Biometric QR Relay

When logging into a website on your desktop PC, the browser extension projects an encrypted QR code. Scan it with your phone to authenticate without typing passwords on your keyboard.

As soon as your camera decodes the QR, the browser displays intermediate visual feedback. Confirming with your fingerprint or face immediately transmits the one-time encrypted payload over an ephemeral WebSocket channel.

  • • Immune to desktop hardware and software keyloggers
  • • Ephemeral rooms self-destruct after consumption or 5-minute timeout
  • • Auto-fill and optional automatic form submission
E2EE P2P Transfer • Auto-Purge Timers

Encrypted Credential Sharing

Share sensitive logins securely with verified family members, team colleagues, or trusted contacts without sending plaintext passwords via email or chat apps.

Configure auto-delete expiration timers ranging from 1 hour to 30 days. FlakeSecure also supports a proprietary Hidden View Mode, allowing the recipient to autofill logins on their browser without ever seeing the plaintext password in their vault.

  • • One-time AES ephemeral transfer encryption
  • • Auto-purge timers (1h, 1d, 7d, 30d, or one-time use)
  • • Hidden view mode protection prevents password copying
RFC 6238 TOTP Engine

Built-in 2FA Authenticator

Full replacement for separate authenticator apps. Store your 2FA seeds alongside your credentials with live 30-second countdown rings. During desktop login, stream your verification codes straight to your browser with a single tap.

Fleet Telemetry • Remote Revocation

Connected Fleet Management

Monitor and audit all active devices connected to your master identity from the web console. Inspect device fingerprints, geolocation, and last access times. Terminate or remotely revoke lost devices instantly.

Web Embed • Drop-In SDK

For Website Providers

Web developers can integrate FlakeSecure biometric QR logins directly into existing web pages using a lightweight 1-line script tag or customizable iframe widget. Works seamlessly with React, Vue, Angular, and Svelte.

30-Day Compliance Retention

Protected Account Retention

If account deactivation is requested, active sessions are revoked instantly and data is moved into a 30-day protected soft-delete retention grace period before cryptographic permanent purge, preventing accidental data loss.

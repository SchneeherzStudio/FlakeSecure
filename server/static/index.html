🔒 Secure Storage

Protected Vault

Biometric Authorization

Point your camera at the browser extension QR code, confirm with your fingerprint or Face ID, and you're logged into your account in under a second.

🛡️ P2P Sync & Share

Zero-Knowledge Sharing

Transfer credentials securely device-to-device with custom expiration timers (1 hour to 30 days), allowed recipient whitelists, and restricted viewing modes.

Zero-Knowledge Guarantee

Cryptographic Architecture

Engineered from the ground up so that even if the relay server were compromised, your passwords remain 100% unbreakable.

🛡️

Client-Side Encryption

All encryption keys are generated randomly on your device using a cryptographically secure random number generator (CSPRNG). Credentials are encrypted with AES-256-CTR and authenticated with HMAC-SHA256 before leaving your phone.

🔐

Argon2id Master Keys

Account master passwords are encrypted with memory-hard Argon2id hashing algorithm with unique cryptographic salts, rendering GPU/ASIC brute-force and rainbow table attacks computationally infeasible.

⚡

Ephemeral In-Memory Relay

The server operates strictly as a zero-knowledge communication relay. Sessions are held temporarily in-memory with a 5-minute time-to-live (TTL) and are permanently wiped immediately upon delivery.

👁️

Restricted Visibility Mode

Share credentials with team members or temporary devices where the recipient can authenticate into websites without being able to inspect, copy, or view the plaintext password.

📲

Hardware Biometric Storage

Your master keys and encrypted vault records are shielded by your smartphone's Secure Enclave / Android Keystore, requiring physical biometric validation (Face ID, Touch ID, BiometricPrompt) to unlock.

🗑️

GDPR Instant Right to Erasure

Full compliance with Art. 17 GDPR. With a single tap in the app's danger zone, your entire account, cryptographic session tokens, logs, and sharing permissions are purged forever via database cascade delete.

How FlakeSecure Works

Say goodbye to typing complex master passwords or trusting cloud-hosted password vaults.

01
💻

Extension Displays QR

Open any login page on your desktop browser. The FlakeSecure browser extension automatically detects the login fields and generates a unique single-use cryptographic QR code.

02
📲

Scan & Biometric Confirm

Open the FlakeSecure mobile app or scan with your device camera. Confirm the website domain with a quick fingerprint or Face ID scan.

03
⚡

Instant Auto-Login

Your encrypted credentials are securely relayed to your browser socket. The extension decrypts the payload client-side, autofills the login form, and logs you in instantly.

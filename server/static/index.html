Core Security

Engineered for Frictionless Privacy

No plaintext passwords ever touch the server. Complete control stays on your physical devices.

Zero-Knowledge Vault

Client-side 256-bit AES encryption derived via PBKDF2 & Argon2id. Even if the server were intercepted, your vault remains mathematically unbreakable.

1-Tap Biometric Relay

Scan a QR code on your browser, authorize via Face ID or fingerprint, and your logins are autofilled in under a second over ephemeral encrypted websockets.

Built-in 2FA Authenticator

Integrated RFC 6238 TOTP engine with live countdowns. Directly stream 2FA verification codes to your desktop browser with a single tap.

Biometric Master Vault

Securely organize logins, categories, and 2FA tokens protected by your phone's Secure Enclave.

Instant Authorization

Confirm domain authorization with biometrics. Encrypted payloads self-destruct after consumption.

Secure P2P Sharing

Send credentials with hidden view modes and auto-delete timers (1h to 30d) across verified recipients.
